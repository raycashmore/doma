# Doma

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

Web, iOS and Android are intended product experiences with platform-specific
design. Responsive web and installable PWA shells support phones and laptops.

## Users

Household members managing everyday household administration on phones and
laptops.

## Product Purpose

Doma is a private operating system for everyday household life. It brings
plans, finances, lists, meals and obligations into focused apps with shared
identity and data. Success means less effort keeping household life coordinated.

## Operating Context

Household information arrives through calendars, financial captures, shared
lists and forwarded notices. Members need to review what matters, coordinate
with one another and act on upcoming obligations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home provides a household noticeboard with shared notes and source-aware cards.
- Budget provides financial trends and account views from source captures.
- Schedule presents the shared household week.
- Lists supports personal and shared structured checklists.
- Meals supports recipes and weekday meal planning with reviewable AI suggestions.
- Briefings and reminders help surface actionable household obligations.
- Focused apps share identity, data and navigation while retaining independent
  framework and deployment boundaries.
- Offline support has explicit limits: cached web shells do not imply live data
  or edits are available offline. Full offline synchronisation is deferred.
- Linear owns priorities, scope and delivery status; this file records durable
  product context rather than a roadmap.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named Doma, a Czech word meaning “at home.”

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains implemented app surfaces, generic fixtures and examples,
and product and architecture documentation linked from README.md. It has no
public demo. Existing code is evidence of implementation, not proof that every
capability has passed acceptance in its intended environment.

## Product Principles

- Reduce the effort of coordinating everyday household life.
- Keep each task focused while making the apps feel like one product.
- Preserve private household data; use generic examples in repository artifacts.
- Keep source facts distinguishable from derived views and AI suggestions.
- Represent connectivity and offline limits honestly.
